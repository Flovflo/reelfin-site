A few things
to know.

Do I need a Jellyfin server?

Yes. ReelFin is a client for your own Jellyfin server, or a server you are authorised to use. Enter the server address and sign in to your Jellyfin account.

Does ReelFin include movies or shows?

No. You bring your library. The screenshots and poster artwork on this page use a fictional demonstration collection; no movies or shows are supplied with the app.

Will every file Direct Play?

Direct Play depends on the file, codecs, subtitles and device. ReelFin uses native Apple playback, and Jellyfin may remux or transcode a file when it needs help.

Can I watch offline?

On iPhone and iPad, you can download compatible titles when your Jellyfin account allows downloads. File compatibility and available storage still apply. Downloads are not available on Apple TV.

How does ReelFin handle privacy?

Your session token is stored in Apple Keychain. ReelFin does not sell your personal data or use cross-app tracking. Optional crash diagnostics may be enabled in a production build. Read the privacy policy.

Is ReelFin available on Mac?

A dedicated Mac experience is in development. It is not available for public download yet. ReelFin is currently available for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.
